const std::vector< Platform * > &  platforms

Updates all wigglers in wigglers_, fills newWigglers_ with new wigglers to add, and fills toRemoveWigglers_ with wigglers that have been killed.

Also moves all wigglers from newWigglers to wigglers_ and emptys newWigglers. As well as removing all wigglers in wigglers_ that are in toRemoveWigglers_, then empties toRemoveWigglers_.
